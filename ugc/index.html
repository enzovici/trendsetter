About me

Hi, I'm Camila, a beauty UGC creator and creative producer based in Milan.

I make warm, honest short-form videos and photos for beauty, home and pet brands: filmed at home, styled with fresh flowers, often with my cat stealing the scene.

I grew up in Uzbekistan and work in Russian, English and Italian, so one shoot can run in three markets. I'm finishing a master's in marketing, which is why I script for the hook and the third second, not only for the look.

Why me?

A soft, styled eye and real-life moments people actually stop for, from a marketer who knows what you are testing. Content that feels like a friend's recommendation, in three languages.

The concept

Creative idea

A candle that smells like the morning a bouquet arrives. I built the campaign around that first hour: paper unwrapped on the kitchen table, wet stems on marble, peonies opening in the light, and the candle already burning among them. The cat walks through the set once, because that is what really happens at home.
